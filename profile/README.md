Your analytics shows people. AI agents such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini do not run its JavaScript, so they never show up there. Knocklog reads what reaches your server and tells you:

- **Who visits:** training crawlers, AI search crawlers, assistants fetching a page for a person, agentic browsers.
- **Who is real:** anyone can claim to be GPTBot. Impostors are flagged, with proof.
- **What they read:** the pages agents fetch most, and whether your site is ready for them (robots.txt, llms.txt, content readable without JavaScript).
- **What they bring:** people sent by AI assistants, and the forms and WhatsApp contacts they turn into.

It works on any hosting: Cloudflare, cPanel and shared hosting, WordPress, Linux servers with Nginx or Apache, Next.js on Vercel or Netlify, and Node.js.

### For AI agents

Knocklog has an MCP server, so Claude, ChatGPT and other agents can check a site, identify a request or read your report in the conversation.

- Public connector, no sign-in: `https://api.knocklog.ai/mcp/directory`
- Listed in the [official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io) as `io.github.leonlesenechal/knocklog`
